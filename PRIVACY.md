# Country Filter for X: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Country Filter for X is a free browser extension from TechyGeeksHome. It hides or blurs posts on x.com based on where the author's account is based, using the "Account based in" information X shows on each profile.

## What it stores
Everything is kept in your own browser, using the browser's extension storage:
- your chosen countries and regions, and your settings
- a cache of accounts it has checked (handle, display name, "Account based in", "Connected via", when it was checked, and whether you blocked it from the extension)
- counts of how many posts were filtered from each location

Cached accounts expire after 30 days. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

## What it sends, and where
The extension only talks to x.com, using your own signed-in session:
- it asks x.com for the "About this account" details of accounts whose posts are on your screen
- when you click Block, it asks x.com to block that account on your X account

Nothing is sent to TechyGeeksHome or to anyone else. There are no analytics, no adverts, no tracking and no accounts.

## What it doesn't do
- It does not read your messages, passwords or anything outside x.com and twitter.com.
- It does not sell or share any data.
- It does not block anyone unless you click Block yourself.
